Treehouse Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

The short version: Treehouse has no ads and no tracking. Kids don't make accounts. The game saves progress on the device, and a grown-up can choose to back it up with their Google account. Chat messages and nicknames are never uploaded.

Who we are

Treehouse is a game for children aged 8 to 12, made by a young creator with a parent. The parent is responsible for the app and this policy. Contact: [your dad's email]

What Treehouse saves on the device

  • Game progress: coins, shop items, unlocked packs, your penguin's look, your hut, stamps, quests, treasures and best scores.
  • A nickname, if the child types one into chat. It stays on the device only.

This information is stored in the browser or app on the device. It is not sent to us.

Optional cloud backup (grown-ups only)

A grown-up can sign in with Google in the PIN-protected Grown-up area to back up game progress. If they do:

  • Google Firebase stores the game progress listed above, linked to that Google account.
  • Google shares the grown-up's account ID and email address with Treehouse so sign-in works.
  • Chat messages and nicknames are never uploaded.
  • Each account can only read its own save.

What Treehouse does not do

  • No ads, and no advertising trackers.
  • No analytics or tracking tools.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • We don't sell or share anyone's information.

Chat and the other penguins

The other penguins in Treehouse are computer characters. Children cannot chat with real people. A safety filter blocks unkind words and personal information, such as phone numbers and addresses.

Purchases

Optional purchases (the Island Pass, style packs and the Everything bundle) are behind a grown-up check and are handled by Apple. Treehouse never sees card or payment details. The app only learns which items were bought, so it can unlock them.

Deleting data

  • Grown-ups can reset progress with "Start over" in the Grown-up area.
  • To delete a cloud backup, email us at the address above and we will delete it.

Children's privacy

Treehouse is made for children. We collect as little as possible, and nothing that identifies a child. If you think a child's personal information has been shared with us, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
